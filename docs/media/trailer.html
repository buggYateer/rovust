Sensing

Twenty sensors.
Each one placed for a reason.

  • 8 sonars around the body
  • 3 infrared beams, forward
  • 6 cliff switches, front · side · rear
  • gyro + compass or sun sensor
  • camera with terrain AI
  • ground-speed sensor, battery, gas, light

Three worlds · one brain

The same robot. Only the configuration changes.

Cave

Branching corridors, darkness, pits and gas. Short sonar range, headlights, a compass disturbed by iron.

Mars

Open terrain, rock fields, sand that makes the wheels slip. No magnetic field: a sun sensor gives heading.

Underwater

Reef walls and current. Thrusters instead of wheels, sonar instead of light, a Doppler log instead of encoders.
